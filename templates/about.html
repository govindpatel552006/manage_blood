About This System

A comprehensive blood bank management platform built to connect donors, hospitals, and administrators seamlessly.

🩸

Live Inventory

Real-time blood stock tracking with automatic low-stock alerts when units fall below 20.

🏥

Hospital Portal

Hospitals can submit blood requests, check availability, and track request approval status.

👤

Donor Records

Complete donor management with blood type, medical history, and donation tracking.

✅

Request Approval

Admin approves or rejects requests. Inventory auto-deducts upon approval.

📊

Admin Dashboard

Central overview of all donors, hospitals, inventory, and pending requests.

🔒

Role-Based Access

Separate portals for admins and hospitals with secure session management.
